About Us

Our Story

Cozy Cup Café started with a simple idea of creating a warm and comfortable place where people could enjoy fresh coffee and delicious food.

Over the years, our café has become a friendly place for students, families, and coffee lovers. We focus on fresh ingredients, quality coffee, and a welcoming environment. Our goal is to make every visit comfortable and memorable.
